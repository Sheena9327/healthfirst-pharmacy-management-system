# 💊 HealthFirst Pharmacy Management System

### Business Analysis | Database Design | Process Improvement

HealthFirst Pharmacy is an end-to-end pharmacy management system designed to centralize prescription processing, inventory management, customer information, employee operations, and delivery tracking within a structured database solution.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

Pharmacy operations involve multiple interconnected processes including prescriptions, medication inventory, customer records, payments, employees, and deliveries.

Reliance on manual or disconnected processes can create:

- Limited visibility into medication inventory
- Inefficient prescription processing
- Difficulty tracking customer and operational information
- Increased risk of manual errors
- Data security and compliance challenges

The goal of **HealthFirst Pharmacy** was to design a centralized database-driven solution that could improve information accessibility, operational efficiency, and data management.

---

## 💡 Solution

Designed a relational pharmacy management system that translates business requirements into structured database entities, relationships, business rules, and operational workflows.

The solution supports:

- 💊 Prescription and medication management
- 📦 Inventory and stock tracking
- 👥 Customer information management
- 🧑‍💼 Employee management
- 🚚 Delivery tracking
- 🔐 Role-based access and data security
- 📊 Structured operational data for reporting and analysis

---

## 📈 Project Impact

**Designed a centralized pharmacy management database spanning multiple operational functions by translating business requirements into relational data structures, business rules, entity relationships, and a Microsoft Access implementation.**

The project demonstrates how structured data management can replace fragmented manual processes and provide a foundation for more reliable operational reporting and decision-making.

## 🔄 Business Analysis Approach

**1. Identified the Business Problem**  
Analyzed operational challenges associated with managing pharmacy information across prescriptions, inventory, customers, employees, and deliveries.

**2. Defined Business Requirements**  
Translated operational needs into system requirements and documented business rules.

**3. Designed the Data Model**  
Defined entities, attributes, primary keys, foreign keys, and relationships required to support pharmacy operations.

**4. Developed the Database**  
Implemented the relational database using Microsoft Access and SQL.

**5. Considered Security & Reliability**  
Incorporated concepts including role-based access, audit logging, backup and recovery, and SQL injection prevention.
